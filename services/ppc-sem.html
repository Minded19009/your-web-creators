The thinking behind the work

Paid attention. Earned interest.

Ad spend gets expensive when the message, audience, and landing page are having different conversations. We bring them together, set up useful measurement where your consent and technical setup allow it, and keep decisions connected to the commercial goal.

Every engagement starts with a written scope. We agree the deliverables, feedback process, and dependencies before the work begins, so the interesting surprises stay in the creative.

01

How much should we spend on ads?

+

That depends on your market, offer, conversion economics, and how quickly you need to learn. We propose a media budget during discovery. The listed agency fee does not include ad spend or guarantee a volume of leads.

02

Who owns the finished work?

+

With upfront payment, transferable rights in approved original deliverables pass after full payment. Under Monthly Rental, you receive a usage license during the 12-month term and transferable rights after all scheduled payments. Third-party assets retain their own licenses. Domains and platform accounts should be registered to your business.

03

How many revisions are included?

+

Most project packages include two consolidated refinement rounds; ongoing content includes one review round per monthly batch. The proposal states the exact allowance. If the brief changes or a new direction is needed, we agree the additional scope before doing the work.

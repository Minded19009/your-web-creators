Shine Elite

01 / The challenge

Premium service.
Ordinary presentation.

Shine Elite had built a real reputation for careful, consistent cleaning across homes and offices — but their digital presence didn't reflect that standard. The existing site read like a local services directory: generic stock imagery, a wall of undifferentiated features, and a contact button that asked people to commit before they had any reason to trust the business. For a service that competes on trust and reliability, the visual and verbal gap between the quality of the work and the quality of the website was the problem worth solving. Prospective customers arriving from a referral or a search had no clear signal that this was a different kind of cleaning company — one that treats detail seriously and treats clients like adults.

02 / The approach

Quiet confidence.
Earned through detail.

We built the direction around the idea that premium service doesn't need to shout. The visual language is calm and considered: a restrained palette, generous whitespace, and typography that reads like it means what it says. Photography and illustration are used sparingly — one strong idea per section rather than a collage of cleaning imagery. Structurally, the site answers the questions that actually drive bookings. What's included, how often, how much, and who will show up. Testimonials sit next to the service they support rather than isolated on a separate page. Pricing is visible early, and the primary call to action is consistent across every scroll depth. Nothing is hidden behind a form or a phone call that shouldn't be.

Service-first structure with clear scope and frequency options

Visible pricing and transparent add-ons

Testimonials and trust signals placed beside the services they support

03 / The build

A site that reads
like the service feels.

The final build is a clean, fast, responsive site with a consistent brand voice across every page. Navigation is straightforward, the booking path is short, and the mobile experience was treated as the primary design target rather than an afterthought — because most cleaning bookings start on a phone. The interaction layer is deliberately restrained. Hover states are considered, transitions are smooth, and nothing moves without a reason. The result is a site that behaves the way Shine Elite's work behaves: unhurried, precise, and quietly confident.

Premium isn't louder. It's more careful.

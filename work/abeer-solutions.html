Abeer

01 / The challenge

Deep expertise.
Shallow first impression.

Abeer Solutions has real depth. A certified Microsoft partner with two decades of hands-on experience across Dynamics AX, GP, Business Central, Dynamics 365 Finance and Operations, and Odoo — implementing, upgrading, and supporting ERP for companies across Saudi Arabia. They also handle the regulatory side that decides whether an ERP project actually succeeds in the Kingdom: ZATCA Phase 2 e-invoicing, VAT, corporate tax, withholding tax. The problem was the website didn't say any of that convincingly. A prospective buyer — usually a CFO, ops lead, or IT director evaluating three or four partners — would land on the site and come away with a vague impression rather than a specific one. The credibility was real. It just wasn't being communicated in the first sixty seconds.

02 / The approach

Lead with proof.
Not adjectives.

We rebuilt the site around the questions a serious ERP buyer actually asks. What do you implement? Which platforms? What's your experience level? How do you handle Saudi compliance? What does delivery look like, and what happens after go-live? Each of those questions now has a clear, specific answer on the page — not buried in a brochure PDF, but visible and scannable on a screen. The visual direction is deliberately restrained. Enterprise software websites in the region tend to over-decorate — too many stock photos of handshakes, too many gradient buttons, too much "digital transformation" language. We went the other way. Deep navy, warm accent, clean typography, generous whitespace, and heavy use of the platform's own Dynamics and Odoo product names as the primary design element. The site now reads the way the business actually works: precise, informed, and confident without being loud.

Product-led structure across the full Dynamics 365 family and Odoo

A dedicated compliance section covering ZATCA, VAT, WHT, and Zakat

An FAQ that answers the questions buyers actually search for

03 / The build

A site that earns
the first meeting.

The final build is a fast, responsive, single-language site with a clear navigation structure, strong SEO foundations, and a consistent tone across every page. Service pages, platform pages, and the compliance section are all built to be individually linkable — so a salesperson can send a specific URL to a specific prospect with a specific problem. That sounds small. For an enterprise partner, it's the difference between "have a look at our site" and "here's exactly the thing you asked about." The interaction layer stays out of the way. Smooth scroll, subtle hover states, and accordion FAQs that expand without drama. No animation for animation's sake. The result is a site that behaves the way Abeer's engagements behave: senior, methodical, and reliably delivered.

Credibility isn't claimed. It's demonstrated in the details.

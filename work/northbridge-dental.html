Northbridge Dental Studio.

Dentistry that feels different.

Engagement

Clinic website & digital direction

Disciplines

The intention

Make a premium Marylebone dental studio feel calm, human, and easy to book

View live site

Northbridge

01 / The challenge

Clinical quality.
A patient experience that starts on the homepage.

Northbridge Dental Studio is a private practice in Marylebone, founded in 2017, offering general, cosmetic, orthodontic, and implant dentistry in a studio designed to feel less like a clinic and more like a calm, considered space. The clinicians are genuinely senior, the treatment philosophy is conservative and transparent, and the practice runs on a membership plan rather than insurance-first — which means patients typically stay for years. The clinical quality was never the issue. The problem was the website. It read like a corporate dental chain — stock photography of smiling patients, a wall of treatment names, pricing buried three clicks deep, and a "book an appointment" form that promised a callback rather than a booking. For a studio whose entire positioning is "dentistry that feels different," the site felt exactly like the one everyone else had. Anxiety-friendly, in particular, was being claimed on the website and completely undermined by the website itself.

02 / The approach

Calm first.
Clarity everywhere.

We built the direction around the practice's own tone: calm, warm, quietly expert. The palette is soft navy, sage-teal, and warm honey — clinical without being cold — with DM Serif Display for headings and DM Sans for the interface, a family pairing that keeps the whole site feeling of a piece. The homepage uses a split hero rather than a full-bleed one, deliberately signalling calm rather than spectacle. That choice alone sets the tone before a single treatment name is read. The structure follows the same philosophy. The membership plan — the practice's strongest differentiator — gets its own homepage block and a full page on the pricing section, rather than being buried as a footnote. Before/after sliders appear on the cosmetic, orthodontic, and implant pages because that's the honest way to show real work. The emergency strip runs above the footer on every page, with a same-day slot promise, because that's what patients in pain actually need to find. And the pricing page is treated as a first-class page rather than a hidden appendix, because "transparent pricing" means very little if the pricing is two clicks away.

Membership-plan-led pricing page with full treatment price list

03 / The build

Approachable.
Accessible by default.

The build is a static site with a keyboard-accessible before/after slider (arrow keys move the handle), accordion FAQs on each treatment page, a booking form with client-side validation and a clear success message, and a stylized inline-SVG map of the Marylebone location — no third-party maps, no tracking scripts. The emergency strip is visible above the footer on every page, with a click-to-call phone number that works on mobile. Motion is calm and restrained. Split headlines reveal line by line. Before/after sliders drag smoothly with full keyboard control. Accordions open without drama. Headers solidify on scroll. Every animation respects prefers-reduced-motion, and nothing in the motion layer gets between a nervous patient and the information they came to find. The result is a clinic website that behaves the way the clinic itself does: unhurried, clear, and genuinely reassuring.

For a nervous patient, calm is a design decision.

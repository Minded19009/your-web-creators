Sunshine Ducts

01 / The challenge

Real service.
An industry that's hard to trust.

Sunshine Ducts provides duct, vent, furnace, AC, and dryer vent cleaning for homes and businesses across the USA. The service offering is legitimate — trained technicians, professional equipment, careful work in client spaces, honest pricing. But the category itself is one of the least trusted in home services. A history of aggressive door-to-door sales, high-pressure upsells, and fly-by-night operators has trained consumers to be suspicious of anyone offering duct cleaning. That's the problem a website has to solve before it can sell anything. Sunshine Ducts' previous site didn't solve it. It had the same suspicious tropes as everyone else: a stock photo of a technician with a clipboard, "why duct cleaning matters" copy that read like a scare tactic, and a pricing table that required a phone call to understand. For a company trying to position itself as the honest, transparent option in a dishonest category, the site was working against it. The brief was clear: build a site that treats customers like adults and explains exactly what's being done, why, and how much.

02 / The approach

Transparency.
No scare tactics.

We built the direction around three decisions. First, transparency as the brand. The homepage leads with a clear promise ("Breathe cleaner air with professional duct and vent cleaning"), a trust strip (trained, background-checked, transparent scope, no surprises), then a clear explanation of what's actually being cleaned and why. No fear-based copy. No "your family could be breathing toxic mold right now." Just the honest case for regular maintenance. Second, scoped language everywhere. Every service is scoped with clear terms: "we clean accessible ductwork, vents, and equipment" — because a service business that's honest about what it can't do is more trustworthy, not less. Third, the pricing page works. It presents a 3-tier monthly/yearly plan structure with real prices ($19.99/mo through $239.99/mo) and clear inclusion lists, so customers can understand the cost without a phone call. The design language is warm and confident — a bright blue and warm gold palette with a clean sans-serif pairing that reads as friendly but professional. It's designed to feel like a service you could trust in your home, not like a sales pitch.

03 / The build

Built to answer
every real question.

The build is a static site with a detailed services page covering four cleaning types (ducts and vents, furnace and AC, dryer vent, full system inspection), a pricing page with monthly/yearly toggle and real prices, a "how it works" process section that walks through the three steps (inspection and scope, professional cleaning, final walkthrough), a customer reviews section with real-looking testimonials, and a trust-building about page. Every service page carries the same scoped-language discipline — it says what's cleaned and what isn't, because that honesty is the entire brand. Motion is warm but restrained. The stat counters count up on scroll. Hover states on cards and buttons are subtle. The header solidifies on scroll. The before/after visual on the homepage gives an honest sense of the difference without exaggeration. Every animation respects prefers-reduced-motion, and nothing in the motion layer undermines the transparent tone. The result is a site that behaves the way Sunshine Ducts behaves: careful, honest, and clearly worth recommending.

In a category that's lost trust, honesty is the differentiator.

Peace Destinations

01 / The challenge

Trusted agency.
Website that didn't convert.

The Peace Destinations is an independent travel agency in Nelson, Lancashire, arranging holidays, cruises, Umrah and Hajj trips. The team is genuinely experienced — long-standing relationships with travel partners, in-house management of the Umrah and Hajj side, and the kind of personal service that online travel agents can't replicate. They're the agency locals recommend to their neighbours, their families, and their friends. The reputation is real. The website was a different story. It was cluttered with a dozen competing calls to action, half-finished packages, and copy that read like it had been assembled from three different brochures. Visitors — often people booking the most important trip of their year — had to work hard to understand what the agency actually offered, whether they could afford it, and how to take the first step. For a business whose entire pitch is "we take the stress out of planning," the site was adding stress rather than removing it. The brief was to build a clean, focused website that reflected the agency's actual warmth and competence.

02 / The approach

Warm, focused,
built around trust.

We built the direction around three decisions. First, a warm and inviting aesthetic — deep teal, warm sand, and soft gold accents with a modern serif for headings and a clean sans for the interface. It reads like a travel agent you'd actually walk into on the high street, not like a discount booking platform or a corporate travel conglomerate. Second, the structure follows how customers actually plan trips. The homepage leads with a clear promise ("Let's plan where to next?"), a "Make Your Trip" customisation tool that invites visitors to share their details for a quote, and a featured packages section for the most popular destinations. The Umrah and Hajj side gets its own dedicated space — because it's not just another service category, it's the reason many customers choose the agency in the first place. Third, the site emphasises trust at every step. Clear pricing guidance, honest explanations of cancellation and payment terms, and a "why choose us" section that names real advantages (long-standing travel partners, in-house Umrah management, direct phone access) rather than vague promises. The copy is warm and direct. "Begin your peaceful journey" — that's the promise, and every page delivers on it. No "unlock your next adventure" or "curated travel experiences." Just honest language about what the agency actually does.

03 / The build

Built to convert
and reassure.

The build is a static site with dedicated pages for holidays, cruises, Umrah, and Hajj, a "Make Your Trip" quote request tool that collects destination, dates, budget, and group size, a featured packages carousel, a "why choose us" page that names real advantages (partners, in-house management, direct contact), and a contact page with click-to-call phone numbers for both UK and international lines. Every page is designed to move a browser closer to a booking, without ever feeling pushy. Motion is warm but restrained. The packages carousel scrolls smoothly. Stat counters count up on scroll. Hover states on cards and CTAs are subtle. The header solidifies on scroll. Every animation respects prefers-reduced-motion, and nothing in the motion layer gets in the way of a family finding the trip they're looking for. The result is a website that behaves the way the agency behaves: warm, knowledgeable, and quietly confident.

For a travel agency, trust is the destination.

Avante Pharmacy.

Trusted local pharmacy, made easier to reach.

Engagement

Pharmacy website & digital direction

Disciplines

Positioning, UX/UI, responsive design, service discovery

The intention

Give a busy Edinburgh pharmacy a site that helps patients find and book services without friction

View live site

Avante

01 / The challenge

A busy pharmacy.
A website that hid its services.

Avante Pharmacy sits on Westfield Road in Edinburgh and offers both NHS dispensing and a wide range of private services — travel vaccines, private blood testing, vitamin B12 injections, shingles and HPV vaccines, and professional microsuction ear wax removal. It's a genuinely useful local pharmacy: family-run, well-stocked, easy to walk into, and staffed by people who actually know their patients. The service offering is broader than most pharmacies nearby, and the pricing is fair. The website did not communicate any of that. It was a single-page site with a services carousel, a "call us" phone number, and copy that read like a pharmaceutical brochure — clinical, dense, and unclear about what could actually be booked online versus what required walking in. Patients searching "ear wax removal Edinburgh" or "travel vaccines near me" would land on the site, get confused about whether they could book, and call the competition. For a pharmacy trying to grow its private services (the higher-margin part of the business), the website was a leaky bucket. The brief was to build a proper website that made each service clear, bookable, and easy to find.

02 / The approach

Warm, clear,
quietly professional.

We built the direction around three decisions. First, a warm and clinical aesthetic — a green, cream, and warm grey palette with a clean sans-serif pairing that reads as trustworthy and approachable rather than cold or corporate. For a pharmacy, that balance matters: patients need to trust the clinical competence, but they also need to feel welcome walking in. Second, the structure follows how patients actually search for services. The homepage leads with the pharmacy's core promise, a quick "popular services" grid that names the most-searched services upfront, and clear opening hours immediately visible. Every service has its own page explaining what it is, who it's for, how to book, and how much it costs — because for private services, clarity about price is the entire differentiator. Third, the booking flow is honest. Some services are bookable online, some require a phone call, and the site is explicit about which is which. That transparency builds trust and reduces wasted phone calls. The copy is deliberately plain-spoken. No pharmaceutical jargon, no clinical hedging. "A professional microsuction earwax removal service at our pharmacy" — that's the tone. Useful, clear, and patient-friendly.

03 / The build

Built to answer
every real question.

The build is a static site with six dedicated service pages (travel vaccines, private blood testing, vitamin B12 injections, specialty vaccines, ear wax removal, NHS repeat prescriptions), a proper FAQ section on each service page, an about page with the pharmacy's story and team, a contact page with opening hours and a stylized inline-SVG map of the Edinburgh location, and a service finder that helps patients identify which service they need before booking. Every service page is honest about what's included, who it's suitable for, and how much it costs — because for a private pharmacy service, opacity is the biggest barrier to conversion. Motion is warm but restrained. Hover states on service cards and CTAs are subtle. Stat counters count up on scroll. The header solidifies on scroll. The FAQ accordions open smoothly. Every animation respects prefers-reduced-motion, and nothing in the motion layer gets in the way of a patient finding a service, a price, or a way to book. The result is a pharmacy website that behaves the way Avante behaves: warm, clear, and quietly professional.

For a pharmacy, clear beats clever.

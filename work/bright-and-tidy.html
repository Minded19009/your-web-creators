Bright & Tidy

01 / The challenge

Good service.
No way to book it.

Bright & Tidy Co. has been cleaning Dubai homes and small offices since 2018. The team is vetted, insured, uniformed, and trained on eco-friendly products. Customers tend to stay for years. In a market flooded with operators who treat cleaning as a side hustle, Bright & Tidy had built something genuinely different: a business that shows up on time, every time, and treats a client's home with respect. The website was doing none of that. It was a one-page brochure with a stock photo of a smiling woman holding a mop, three lines of copy, and a phone number. No service pages. No pricing. No reviews. No way to book. For a company trying to move upmarket — into villas, offices, holiday-home turnarounds — the website was actively losing trust. The quality was real. The presentation wasn't. The brief was to build a proper website that would reflect the actual business, convert enquiries at scale, and take some pressure off the phone.

02 / The approach

Warm, trustworthy,
quietly premium.

We built the direction around three decisions. First, a warm and trustworthy aesthetic — deep teal, sky blue, and sunny yellow, with Nunito for headings and DM Sans for the interface. It reads like a business you'd let into your home, not like a discount operator and not like a cold corporate chain. Second, the structure follows how customers actually book cleaning. The homepage leads with a clear promise ("We clean. You breathe."), a trust strip (vetted, insured, eco-friendly, satisfaction guaranteed), a before/after slider showing a real transformation, then a services overview and a pricing snapshot. Someone comparing three cleaning companies on a Friday night can find everything they need in under a minute. Third, the site is built around two interactive tools that do the heavy lifting: a before/after slider showing real transformations, and a live pricing estimator on the pricing page. Select property size, clean type, and frequency — the estimated price updates in real time. No phone call required. No email form begging for information. Just the price, plus a one-click path to booking.

Warm, trustworthy design system with teal/sky/sunny palette

Interactive before/after slider and live pricing estimator

Dedicated booking page with a full-service enquiry form

03 / The build

Every detail
earns its keep.

The build is a static site with a services page covering six cleaning types (regular home, deep clean, move-in/move-out, office and commercial, post-construction, holiday home and Airbnb), a dedicated pricing page with the live estimator and full add-ons list, a proper booking page with a detailed enquiry form, an about page with team members and the eco-product range, a reviews page with filter tabs and aggregate ratings, and a contact page with WhatsApp quick-contact. The pricing estimator updates live as the customer changes property size, clean type, and frequency — no page reload, no phone call, no friction. Motion is warm but restrained. The before/after slider drags smoothly with full keyboard control. The stat counters count up on scroll. Hover states on cards and buttons are subtle and confident. The header solidifies on scroll. Every animation respects prefers-reduced-motion, and nothing in the motion layer gets in the way of a customer finding a price or booking a clean. The result is a website that behaves the way Bright & Tidy behaves: reliable, transparent, and quietly excellent.

For a cleaning business, trust is a design decision.

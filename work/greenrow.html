01 / The challenge

Good service.
Invisible online.

GreenRow Lawn & Landscape is a residential and light commercial grounds maintenance company in Sunderland, founded in 2016. Reliable, well-equipped, friendly — the kind of local business people recommend to their neighbors. The service offering is broad (mowing, hedges, seasonal cleanups, gutter clearing, commercial grounds maintenance) and the pricing is honest and transparent. In a category where most operators are still handing out paper flyers and relying on word of mouth, GreenRow had real operational quality. The problem was that none of it was discoverable. There was no website at all — just a Facebook page with occasional updates and a phone number that appeared in Google Maps. For a business trying to grow beyond referrals, that was the ceiling. Prospective customers searching "lawn care Sunderland" found competitors with better-looking websites, even when those competitors offered inferior service. The brief was to build a proper website that would rank, convert, and reflect the actual quality of the business — without losing the local, human feel that made GreenRow worth recommending in the first place.

02 / The approach

Local, dependable,
quietly professional.

We built the direction around three decisions. First, a friendly but professional aesthetic — deep turf green, fresh cut green, and sunny yellow accents, with Outfit for headings and Source Sans 3 for the interface. It reads like a business you'd trust with your property, not like a discount operator and not like a corporate landscaping chain. Second, the structure follows how customers actually buy lawn care. The homepage leads with a clear promise ("Your lawn, handled"), a trust strip (Vetted, insured, eco-friendly products, satisfaction guaranteed), then a services overview, then a pricing snapshot. Someone scanning on a phone at 9pm can find what they need in under a minute. Third, the site is built around two interactive tools that are the actual differentiator: a before/after slider showing a real transformation, and a live pricing estimator on the pricing page that lets a customer select property size, clean type, and frequency to see an estimated price update in real time. No phone call required. No email form begging for information. Just the price.

03 / The build

Every detail
earns its keep.

The build is a static site with four dedicated service pages (lawn mowing, landscaping, hedge and tree care, commercial grounds), a dedicated pricing page with the live estimator, a booking page with a proper enquiry form, an about page with the team, a reviews page with filtering, and a contact page with a stylized inline-SVG map of the Sunderland service area. The pricing estimator updates live as the customer changes property size, clean type, and frequency — no page reload, no phone call, no friction. Motion is warm but restrained. The before/after slider drags smoothly with full keyboard control. The stat counters count up on scroll. Hover states on cards and buttons are subtle and confident. The header solidifies on scroll. Every animation respects prefers-reduced-motion, and nothing in the motion layer gets in the way of a customer finding a price. The result is a site that behaves the way GreenRow behaves: reliable, transparent, and quietly excellent.

For a local business, trust is built one clear answer at a time.

07 / Fair questions

Glad
you asked.

The useful details, minus the sales fog.

Ask us something else
01

What does Your Web Creators actually do?

+

We bring brand, design, development, and marketing into one coordinated team. You can hire us for a focused project or connect several services around a clear business goal. We start by identifying what will help, not by selling you the entire menu.

02

Who is a good fit for YWC?

+

Founders and teams who value clear thinking, distinctive work, and straightforward collaboration. We work with new businesses and established brands serving their next stage of growth. You bring the business knowledge; we bring the questions and the craft.

03

How does a project start?

+

We begin with a short discovery conversation, then send a written scope covering deliverables, timing, review rounds, responsibilities, and price. Work starts after that scope is accepted and the agreed initial payment is received.

04

How long will the work take?

+

A logo usually takes 2–3 weeks, a brand system 3–5 weeks, a core website 4–6 weeks, and a store 6–8 weeks. A focused app generally takes 8–12 weeks. These are planning ranges; your scope, content readiness, and feedback schedule determine the final timeline.

05

What is the difference between Monthly Rental and One-Time Payment?

+

Monthly Rental spreads the same defined scope across 12 monthly payments at a total cost 25% above the upfront price. One-Time Payment pays that base price upfront. Ongoing marketing, SEO, PPC, and hosting prices cover a defined 12-month service term. They are not unlimited subscriptions.

06

Who owns the finished work?

+

With upfront payment, transferable rights in approved original deliverables pass after full payment. Under Monthly Rental, you receive a usage license during the 12-month term and transferable rights after all scheduled payments. Third-party assets retain their own licenses. Domains and platform accounts should be registered to your business.

07

How many revisions are included?

+

Most project packages include two consolidated refinement rounds; ongoing content includes one review round per monthly batch. The proposal states the exact allowance. If the brief changes or a new direction is needed, we agree the additional scope before doing the work.

08

Can you work with our existing brand or website?

+

Yes. We can work within an established identity, improve a specific journey, or rebuild a site when the existing foundation is limiting the business. We will explain which parts are worth keeping and why.

09

Do you guarantee rankings, sales, or advertising returns?

+

No responsible agency controls those outcomes. We commit to the agreed work, sound execution, clear reporting, and informed recommendations. Market conditions, your offer, budget, competition, and customer behavior all affect results.

10

Do you work with clients outside the US?

+

Yes. We serve clients globally and agree communication windows, review dates, and handoff expectations at the start. Clear written updates do a surprising amount of heavy lifting across time zones.
